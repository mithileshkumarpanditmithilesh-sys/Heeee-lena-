Zero budget se shuruaat ka plan

  1. Ek skill ya topic chunein jisme aap roz practice kar sakein.
  2. Free tutorials se seekhein aur 3–5 sample projects banayein.
  3. Apne kaam ke samples ka portfolio taiyar karein.
  4. Trusted platforms ya local clients par chhote projects ke liye apply karein.
  5. Kaam, payment aur client ki terms pehle clear karein.

Tip: Shuru mein ek hi skill par focus karna, bahut saare earning apps try karne se behtar hai.

Freelancing kaise shuru karein?

Apni ek service chunein — jaise Hindi-English translation, content writing, simple graphic design ya video editing. 3 sample kaam banayein, unhe portfolio mein rakhein aur client ko saaf batayein ki aap kya deliver karenge aur kitne samay mein.

Blogging kaise shuru karein?

Ek focused topic chunein, jaise mobile tips, study help ya beginner skill guides. Aise articles likhein jo asli sawalon ka jawab dein. Copy-paste content se bachein. Readers banne mein samay lagta hai; ads ya sponsorship ki income turant shuru nahi hoti.

Affiliate marketing kaise kaam karti hai?

Affiliate program join karne ke baad aapko special tracking link mil sakta hai. Aap product ke fayde aur kamiyaan imaandari se batayein. Link se eligible purchase hone par commission mil sakta hai. Har post mein affiliate link hone ki baat clearly disclose karein.

Online fraud se kaise bachein?

सावधान रहें: Job paane ke liye registration fee, security deposit ya “guaranteed daily income” ke naam par paise maangne walon se satark rahein. OTP, UPI PIN, password ya bank details kabhi share na karein. Kisi bhi offer ko verify kiye bina payment na karein.
